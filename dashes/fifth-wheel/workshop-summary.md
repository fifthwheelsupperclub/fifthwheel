## What the plan says

The night is a **table**. The app proposes a valid group. Staff approve. Invitations send and do not expire. A seat counts when payment succeeds. Removal asks once for one safe replacement. Voting opens 90 minutes after start, lasts 30 minutes, and shows interest only when mutual. Age comes from date of birth. Guests do not see birthdays or match ranges. A table’s ages span at most 12 years.

Charlie recommends **Table door** (invite-only). Aaron has not selected it. The wireframe draws that recommendation so a builder can see the gates.
