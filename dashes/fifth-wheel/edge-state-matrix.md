# Edge-state matrix — fifth-wheel

**Date:** 2026-10-23  
Each cell names the rendered block in `wireframe.html`.

| Page | Empty | Loading | Error | Permission denied | At scale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your table | `yt-empty` void invitation, pay hidden | `yt-loading` | `yt-error` payment failed, invitation still open | `yt-denied` no invitation for this table | `yt-scale` five names, no extra people and no deadline |
| Take your seat | `pay-empty` price unknown, pay disabled | `pay-loading` processing, not confirmed | `pay-error` card failed, retry pay | `pay-denied` unpaid guest cannot open tonight from here | `pay-scale` same single seat; not a cart |
| Table review | `rev-empty` no draft | `rev-loading` | `rev-error` composition invalid, approve disabled | `rev-denied` guest cannot open staff review | `rev-scale` five rows, ages in years, birthdays not in the table |
| Replacement prompt | `rep-empty` no eligible person, Yes disabled | `rep-loading` | `rep-error` suggestion would break the table, send blocked | `rep-denied` guest cannot remove | `rep-scale` still one name, not a long list |
| Vote | `vote-empty` window closed, no ballot control | `vote-loading` | `vote-error` late submit rejected | `vote-denied` unpaid guest | `vote-scale` four other eligible people, labels visible |
| Mutual reveal | `revl-empty` no mutual pairs, and no one-sided names | `revl-loading` | `revl-error` reveal unavailable, still no one-sided names | `revl-denied` not seated | `revl-scale` a short pair list only |
| Atlanta waitlist | `wl-empty` form before submit | `wl-loading` | `wl-error` under 21 or range invalid | `wl-denied` not used; the form is public | `wl-scale` one form, not a directory of members |

At-scale for a table is five people, not thousands. The waitlist directory is a staff concern and is not drawn as a public list.
