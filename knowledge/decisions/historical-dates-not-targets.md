# Lean-list dates and signup deadlines are not current targets

## Context

The lean list and older landing copy name specific signup deadlines, first-table dates, and waitlist volume goals. The Linear project says those are not current targets. Launch timing is still an open decision.

## Options considered

- Keep the lean-list calendar as the plan
- Treat those figures as historical only until a new launch decision

## Decision and rationale

Historical launch dates and signup deadlines in the source brief are not current project targets. Do not put them on Linear milestones or treat landing “November” copy as a locked date.

## Consequences

Knowledge, issues, and marketing copy must not present those figures as goals. `decision-launch-timing-and-success` is still open.

## Revisit trigger

A new accepted launch decision with dates or success measures.
